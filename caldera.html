Roasting

Roasted to the mineral, not the bean

Volcanic-grown coffee carries more density than most. We roast slower through first crack to let that mineral sweetness come forward instead of burning off.

Every batch is roasted in a 12kg drum and logged by hand. We hold a longer development time after first crack — usually 22–24% of total roast time — which is unusually long for a light-to-medium profile.

The result is a cup with less bright acidity than most single-origins, and a heavier, more mineral finish that holds up in milk as well as it does black.
